# Sample Prompts

A curated set of prompts to try in DiffuseCraft. Copy into the **Prompt** field.

## Photorealistic

- `A cinematic photo of a red fox in a snowy forest, golden hour lighting, shallow depth of field`
- `A cozy cabin in the woods during autumn, warm interior light, photorealistic, 35mm film`
- `Portrait of an elderly fisherman, weathered face, dramatic side lighting, studio photography`

## Digital Art / Concept Art

- `A floating city above the clouds, intricate architecture, concept art, dramatic lighting`
- `A cyberpunk city street at night, neon reflections on wet asphalt, ultra detailed`

## Painterly / Illustrative

## Landscapes

- `A serene Japanese garden with a koi pond and cherry blossoms, soft morning light`

- `Northern lights over a frozen lake, long exposure photography, stars visible`

## Recommended Negative Prompt

```
blurry, low quality, distorted, deformed, disfigured, bad anatomy, watermark, signature, text, extra limbs
```

## Tips

- Add style modifiers (`digital art`, `oil painting`, `35mm photograph`) to steer the aesthetic strongly.
- Add lighting descriptors (`golden hour`, `studio lighting`, `dramatic side lighting`) for more controlled results.
- Increase **CFG scale** (9-12) for stricter prompt adherence; lower it (4-6) for more creative variation.
- Fix a **seed** once you find a composition you like, then iterate on the prompt wording only.
